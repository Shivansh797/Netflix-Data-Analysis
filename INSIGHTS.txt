Conclusion:

This comprehensive analysis reveals that Netflix remains a heavily movie-centric platform, with
feature films comprising roughly 70% of its catalog. The global library experienced an
unprecedented production boom between 2017 and 2020.
Geographically, the United States anchors the platform as the undisputed leader in both film
and television production, prioritizing mature content (TV-MA) centered around documentaries and stand-up comedy.
Interestingly, while India stands out as the second-largest content contributor overall, its production model is almost entirely dominated by movies
rather than multi-season TV shows.Ultimately, the backbone of Netflix's unmatched global appeal relies on a
strategic blend of highly digestible, short-form television (where 1-season formats reign
supreme) paired with universally popular film genres like international dramas, deeply engaging documentaries, and stand-up comedies.
